Mutations

DNA Editor

Drag a base from the tray onto a DNA letter to swap it, or onto a gap between two letters to insert it. Tap a base to select it, then use Delete below to remove it. The mRNA and protein update instantly, and the editor tells you what kind of mutation you made.

Other tools
Keyboard: type A, T, G, or C to swap the selected base. Shift plus a letter inserts. Delete removes. Arrow keys move.
Tip: try to create all 5 kinds of mutation — silent, missense, nonsense, and both kinds of frameshift — to complete every mission above.
