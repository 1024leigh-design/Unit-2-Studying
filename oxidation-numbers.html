Assigning oxidation numbersBonus

Just for the curious: you do not need to know how to assign oxidation numbers for the test. This page shows where the "carbon goes from 0 to +4" and "oxygen goes from 0 to −2" numbers come from.

Four bookkeeping rules

  1. An element by itself (like O₂) is 0.
  2. Hydrogen in a compound is usually +1.
  3. Oxygen in a compound is usually −2.
  4. The numbers in a neutral molecule add up to 0.
